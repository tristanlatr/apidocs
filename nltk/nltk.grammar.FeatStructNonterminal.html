class FeatStructNonterminal(FeatDict, Nonterminal): (source)

Constructor: FeatStructNonterminal(features, **morefeatures)

View In Hierarchy

A feature structure that's also a nonterminal. It acts as its own symbol, and automatically freezes itself when hashed.
